# Review 1: Analysis and Strategic Insight

*Derived from the checklist's "First Review: Analysis and Strategic Insight." Assess against the **brief** from P1.*

This is the heart of the review. A product can be beautifully written, perfectly formatted, and worthless if the analysis doesn't answer the reader's question or advise their decision. Assess **every** item below explicitly against the brief — pass/fail with a one-line reason each.

**Style-standard note (DIA):** if the brief's style standard is DIA, analytic judgments must use words of estimative probability correctly and follow the analytic phrase map. Flag confidence claims that aren't calibrated.

## Completion criteria — assess each

- [ ] **Answers the question asked.** Does the presented analysis directly answer the specific questions the reader asked (from the brief)? If no questions were specified in the brief, does it deliver the topic the brief names?
- [ ] **Advises a decision.** Does the report contain insights that directly advise the reader's decision on an action or point of influence **within their span of control** (per the brief)? Observation is not advice.
- [ ] **No unstated assumptions.** Does the analysis avoid relying on assumptions it never states?
- [ ] **Asserts its basis.** Does the report assert the basis of its findings (e.g., methodology)?
- [ ] **Expertise is sound.** Are we confident in our expertise, or have appropriate subject-matter experts confirmed the observations and insights?
- [ ] **Internal conflicts explained.** Where the report's own conclusions conflict with one another, is each conflict clearly explained?
- [ ] **Cross-references.** If the product shares a topic with other recent internally-written products, does it refer to their findings? *(If corpus access is unavailable, flag this as a human checkpoint rather than asserting a pass.)*
- [ ] **One big idea, cleanly.** If the product is meant to communicate one big idea, does it do so cleanly — free of extraneous or exhaustive findings that belong in a separate product?
- [ ] **Conflicts with prior conclusions.** If findings conflict with conclusions previously presented, is the reason explained?
- [ ] **Conflicts with external belief.** If findings conflict with prominent external research or commonly-held beliefs, is that explained?
- [ ] **No major unanswered questions.** Does the analysis avoid leaving major questions hanging?
- [ ] **Praise the insight.** Are there genuinely insightful elements worth complimenting the authors on? (Name them.)

## Stage output — Analysis assessment

```
## Review 1 — Analysis (assessment)

For each item: PASS / FAIL / N/A + one line.
- Answers the question asked: [PASS/FAIL] — [why]
- Advises a decision: …
- … (all twelve)

**Findings to resolve:** [the FAILs, stated as what the author must address]
**Worth praising:** [the genuinely insightful elements]
```

## Completion gate

The gate passes when every item is assessed (not skipped), the FAILs are stated as actionable findings, and the author confirms: proceed to Stage 2, or revise and resubmit for a re-run of Stage 1.
