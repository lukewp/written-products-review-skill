# Review 10: Publishing *(conditional — public documents only)*

*Derived from the checklist's "Tenth Review: Publishing (if applicable)."*

**Applicability gate.** Run this stage only if the document is being **published for public consumption online**. If not, mark the stage **N/A** and finish the review. Do not skip silently — record N/A.

This section positions a public document to be found in search and cited in AI/LLM prompt responses.

## Completion criteria — assess each

- [ ] **URL slug.** Do keywords in the URL slug signal to LLMs and search engines that the page is worth parsing for relevant prompts and searches?
- [ ] **Citation characteristics.** Assess cited sources from relevant LLM prompt responses. Does this document share the characteristics of content that *gets* cited — a definitions page, a clear purpose paragraph, specific terms of art used correctly? Bring it closer to that shape.
- [ ] **Objectivity signal.** If the document mentions one brand or competitor, consider also mentioning others on a pros/cons grid, so an AI scores it as an objective information source rather than an advocacy piece.
- [ ] **Neutral, provable language.** Eliminate subjective, loaded, emotional, and speculative language to raise the likelihood of citation. *Write what you can prove, not what you feel.* The reader should draw conclusions from the facts — not be led there by adjectives. Keep it tight, keep it neutral.

## An important distinction (flatten the voice, not the finding)

The last item says strip *loaded and emotional* language. It does **not** mean weaken the claims. Removing an intensifier is correct. Turning "X has failed" into "X faces challenges" is not restraint — it is retreat. **Strip the adjectives, not the assertion.** A clinical, declarative statement of a strong finding is exactly what this stage wants; a hedged one is not.

## Completion gate

Passes when each item is assessed (or the stage is marked N/A for a non-public document), loaded-language edits are current/potential pairs that preserve the claim, and the author confirms the review is complete, or resubmits.

## Finishing the review

When the final applicable stage passes, present the **review state** with every gate's disposition (passed / resubmitted / skipped / N/A) so the author has a complete record of what the product cleared.
