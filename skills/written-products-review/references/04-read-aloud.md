# Review 4: Read Aloud

*Derived from the checklist's "Fourth Review: Read out loud."*

The checklist frames this as a human activity: wipe the prior reviews from your mind and read the product out loud to catch what the eye skims past. The computational equivalent is a **readability and rhythm pass** plus a **title/filename check** — approached as if encountering the prose fresh.

## What to do

1. **Approach it cold.** Set aside the findings from Stages 1–3. Read the prose for how it *sounds*, not for what it claims.
2. **Flag awkward language.** Sentences that stumble, tangle, or would make a reader backtrack. Rhythm that drones (every sentence the same length) or lurches. Report each as a **current/potential** pair.
3. **Conversational check.** Does it sound like a person talking to the reader, or like a document performing? Flag the performances.
4. **Read it backwards for typos.** Scan sentence-by-sentence from the end — a mechanical trick that surfaces typos the forward-reading eye auto-corrects.
5. **Title check.** Is the title six words or less and does it make sense? What about the **document title (filename)** — does it, too, communicate the product clearly to someone scanning a folder or inbox?

## Completion criteria — assess each

- [ ] Read cold, independent of prior review findings.
- [ ] Awkward language flagged (current/potential); none left unaddressed.
- [ ] Reads conversationally — performances flagged.
- [ ] Backwards typo scan done.
- [ ] Title (≤6 words) reviewed and sensible.
- [ ] Filename reviewed and communicative.

## Stage output — Read-aloud pass

```
## Review 4 — Read aloud

Awkward / non-conversational (current/potential):
1. Current: "…"  Potential: "…"
Typos caught: […]
Title: "[current]" — [assessment / potential]
Filename: "[current]" — [assessment / potential]
```

## Completion gate

Passes when the cold read is done, awkward lines and typos are surfaced as current/potential pairs, the title and filename are both checked, and the author confirms: proceed to Stage 5, or resubmit.
